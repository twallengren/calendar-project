# US-CORP-IN-VISIBILITY sources

This base calendar composes the US market holiday modules (cited in `sources/US-NYSE/README.md`)
and adds Diwali for visibility.

| id | title | publisher | url / file | retrieved | covers | notes |
|----|-------|-----------|------------|-----------|--------|-------|
| `convention` | Saturday-Sunday weekend | n/a | n/a | n/a | all years | Standard weekend definition; no external source. |
| `india-gazette-holidays` | Central Government holiday lists (gazetted) | Government of India, Ministry of Personnel | https://dopt.gov.in/ (annual holiday circulars) | 2026-02-15 | 2024-2026 | Diwali (Lakshmi Puja) dates; 2027 onward are the widely published projected dates and should be confirmed each year. |
| `nyse-history-2008` | History of New York Stock Exchange Holidays | NYSE | see `sources/US-NYSE/README.md` | 2022-01-14 | 1885-2007 | Cited by the shared US holiday modules this calendar composes. |

This is a visibility calendar rather than a trading or payment calendar. All completeness scopes
are labelled `PROJECTED`; the labels do not turn its notable Diwali rows into market closures or
claim a complete exception history for a real venue.
