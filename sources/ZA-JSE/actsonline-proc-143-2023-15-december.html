Proclamation Notice 143 of 2023

 

by the

 

President of the Republic of South Africa

 

In terms of section 2A of the Public Holidays Act, 1994 (Act No. 36 of 1994), I hereby declare the Fifteenth day of December 2023 as a public holiday throughout the Republic.

 

Given under my Hand and the Seal of the Republic of South Africa at Johannesburg this 22 day of November, Two Thousand and Twenty-Three.

 

 

President

 

 

Minister of Cabinet
