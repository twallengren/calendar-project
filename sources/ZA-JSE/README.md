## Modelling decisions recorded against these sources

- **Every public holiday closes the exchange, and nothing else does.** The JSE's calendars and
  notices mark exactly the statutory holidays of Schedule 1 of the Public Holidays Act 36 of 1994
  plus the days proclaimed under s 2A ("As with all other public holidays in South Africa, the
  JSE will be closed for business on this day", `jse-notice-y1346a-2014`). No full-day closure on
  any other weekday was found 2010-2026.
- **Sunday-to-Monday, never Saturday.** Act s 2(1): "whenever any public holiday falls on a
  Sunday, the following Monday shall be a public holiday"; the calendar's `FORWARD_ONLY` policy
  expresses that, and every exchange calendar read confirms a Saturday holiday brings no
  substitute.
- **A Sunday Christmas gives no automatic Tuesday.** Monday 26 December is already the Day of
  Goodwill, so the Act adds nothing; in 2011, 2016 and 2022 the Tuesday 27 December was
  proclaimed separately under s 2A, and the exchange's own notices show it was not automatic
  (7 October 2011: 27 December "constitute[s] normal business days"; 15 December 2011: "27 December
  2011 has been declared to be a public holiday... accordingly the JSE will be closed"). Christmas
  therefore carries `shift_policy: NONE` and the three Tuesdays are explicit one-offs; the next
  Sunday Christmas, 2033, is outside coverage.
- **Proclaimed holidays.** Election days 18 May 2011, 7 May 2014, 3 August 2016, 8 May 2019,
  1 November 2021, 29 May 2024 and 4 November 2026, and Friday 15 December 2023. For 2011, 2016
  and 2026 the exchange's calendar predates the proclamation and no exchange notice was located;
  those rows rest on the proclamation and the exchange's standing policy.
- **Early closes exist.** Every December the exchange's "Trading, Clearing, Settlement and
  Services Availability" schedule shortens 24 and 31 December, or the preceding business day when
  they fall on a weekend, to a 12:00 close (continuous trading to 11:50, closing auction call
  11:50-12:00). Verified for 2010, 2011 and 2014-2025; the 2012 and 2013 schedules were not
  located, so those two years are `PROJECTED`. One further scheduled early close, Friday 26 April
  2019 at 15:00 before the Integrated Trading and Clearing go-live (`jse-notice-104b-2019`), shows
  the exchange can shorten other days by notice; only the Decembers are `VERIFIED` for early
  closes, the rest of each year `PROJECTED`. The exchange kept normal hours through COVID-19
  ("we will not be shortening trading hours", `jse-hotline-060-2020`).
- **Unscheduled disruptions never shortened a day.** 27 July 2010 opened at 15:05 and closed at
  18:00 (extended, `jse-notice-f4396-2010`); 12 July 2010 lost about 90 minutes intraday; the
  delayed opens of 15 September 2014, 21 September 2017, 18 June 2018 (continuous trading from
  11:00) and 18 August 2021 (afternoon only, `jse-hotline-175-2021`) and the halt of 6 July 2022
  (13:30-15:30, `jse-hotline-101-2022`) all ended with the normal close, and the five-minute pause
  for Nelson Mandela on 6 December 2013 (`jse-notice-171-2013`) likewise; no day of mourning was
  declared. None of these is a calendar event; delayed opens and extended closes are session
  structure. The exchange publishes no monthly trading-day count, so `UNSCHEDULED_EXCEPTIONS`
  stays `PROJECTED`: the incident record is the exchange's own hotlines, but there is no
  independent count reconciliation.
- **Session times.** 09:00-17:00 with the closing auction 16:50-17:00 since at least the
  Millennium Exchange era (2 July 2012) and the November 2013 upgrade (`jse-notice-158b-2013`);
  the 2010-2012 TradElect session table was not located in a primary document.
- **`coverage.verified_through`: 2026-12-31**, the last year with an exchange calendar
  (`jse-notice-380-2025`); 2027-2030 follow the Act.
