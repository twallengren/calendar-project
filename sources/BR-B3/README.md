## Modelling decisions recorded against these sources

- **The exchange's annual holiday circular is the authority.** B3 (BM&FBOVESPA until 2017)
  publishes each year's non-trading days ("não haverá negociação nos mercados de renda variável,
  de derivativos e de renda fixa privada"): archived calendar pages for 2010-2016, Ofícios
  Circulares for 2017-2026 (`b3-calendars`). Every weekday it lists is a closure here and nothing
  else is; the official monthly session counts for 2010-2015
  (`bmfbovespa-numero-mensal-de-pregoes`) equal the modelled calendar in all 72 months, and the
  exchange's stated totals for 2019 (248) and 2021 (247) match. For 2020 the exchange was quoted
  at 248 sessions where the model gives 249; the two mid-year circulars that opened 9 July and
  20 November 2020 account for the model, and the quoted figure is unexplained.
- **No weekend substitution.** A Brazilian holiday on a Saturday or Sunday is not observed on
  another day; the exchange's tables list such days with no replacement, so every fixed-date
  holiday carries `shift_policy: NONE`.
- **'Pontos facultativos' the exchange observes.** Carnival Monday and Tuesday and Corpus Christi
  are optional holidays for the federal service (`br-portaria-mgi-11460-2025`) but closures every
  year; Christmas Eve has no trading session; and the last business day of the year (31 December,
  or the preceding Friday when the 31st falls on a weekend) is listed as "expediente interno nas
  instituições bancárias (sem sessão de negociação)". Ash Wednesday is a 13:00 late open every
  year (pre-opening 12:45-13:00, continuous from 13:00 to the normal close): session structure,
  not a closure, so it carries no row.
- **São Paulo holidays through 2021.** The exchange closed on the city's anniversary
  (25 January) and the state's Constitutionalist Revolution Day (9 July) when they fell on a
  weekday, and on Black Consciousness Day (20 November, municipal) through 2019. It traded on
  9 July and 20 November 2020 after the city and state moved their holidays (`b3-oc-072-2020`) and,
  from the 2022 calendar, on all São Paulo holidays (announced 18 November 2020, cited in
  `b3-ce-109-2021` and `b3-oc-054-2025`). 20 November became a national holiday under Lei
  14.759/2023 and closes the exchange again from 2024 (`b3-oc-215-2023`); Monday 20 November 2023
  was a trading day.
- **One-off closure.** Thursday 12 June 2014, the World Cup opening match in São Paulo, declared
  a municipal holiday (`bmfbovespa-calendar-2014`). The other match days had special hours only.
- **No early closes; no unscheduled closures.** The session is 10:00 to 17:00 or 18:00 depending
  on New York's daylight-saving state (the exchange follows New York since Brazil abolished its
  own daylight saving in 2019; the hours circulars are preserved as `b3-hours-circulars`). The
  circuit breakers of 18 May 2017 and March 2020 (six), the 2010 platform faults, the 1 August
  2025 index-feed failure and the delayed open of 31 July 2026 (equities from about 12:30, normal
  close) were all intraday. The session-count reconciliation covers 2010-2015; from 2016 the
  exception scope rests on the exchange's circulars and the press record, so it is `PROJECTED`.
- **2027 is projected** from the rules and ANBIMA's national list (`anbima-feriados-2027`); the
  exchange had not published its 2027 circular at retrieval.
- **`coverage.verified_through`: 2026-12-31.**
