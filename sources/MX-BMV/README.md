## Modelling decisions recorded against these sources

- **The CNBV's annual list binds the exchange.** Each December the Comisión Nacional Bancaria
  y de Valores publishes in the Diario Oficial the days on which the entities it supervises,
  "bolsas de valores" named among them, "deberán cerrar sus puertas, suspender operaciones"
  (`cnbv-dias-inhabiles`, the 2017-2026 provisions preserved). The exchange's own 2026 table
  (`bmv-calendario-2026`) is identical to that year's list, and the exchange's annual reports
  give session counts for 2010-2012 and 2014-2017 that equal the modelled calendar in every
  year. The list is a superset of the statutory rest days of art. 74 of the Ley Federal del
  Trabajo: it adds Maundy Thursday, Good Friday, 2 November and 12 December.
- **No weekend substitution.** The list gives fixed dates plus "Los sábados y domingos"; a
  fixed-date holiday on a weekend is lost (1 January 2011, 2012, 2017, 2022, 2023; 1 May 2010,
  2011, 2016, 2021, 2022; and so on). The three Monday-rule holidays of the 2006 reform never
  fall on a weekend. Election days (art. 74 IX) and the former 1 December inauguration day all
  fell on weekends within coverage.
- **One-off closures.** Friday 17 September 2010 (the bicentennial, added by the CNBV's amending
  resolution, `cnbv-resolucion-2010-09`) and Tuesday 1 October 2024 (the new inauguration day of
  art. 74 VII, `dof-2023-12-12-dias-2024`); the next inauguration, 1 October 2030, is outside
  coverage.
- **Christmas Eve is a shortened session, announced yearly.** The exchange's own hours are on
  record only for 2018 ("de las 8:00 a las 12:00 horas", `fund-compass-2018-12-18`); the fund
  notices of 2020, 2024 and 2025 cite a change in the exchange's hours and close three hours
  early, consistent with 12:00. The session is modelled at 12:00 from 2018, `VERIFIED` for 2018
  only, `PROJECTED` for the other years from 2018, and `INCOMPLETE` on the five weekday
  Christmas Eves of 2010-2015 for which no notice was found. 31 December is a normal session.
- **Session hours track New York.** The regular session is 8:30-15:00; while the United States
  is on daylight-saving time and Mexico is not (the mismatch weeks before 2023, the whole
  March-October period since Mexico abolished daylight saving in October 2022) the session runs
  7:30-14:00 (`bmv-manual-operativo`, `bmv-horarios-negociacion`, the exchange's notices of 2019-2021
  and the fund notices). Session structure, out of scope.
- **Two outages ended a session early.** 19 September 2017: an administrative recess from 13:43
  to the close after the earthquake (`bmv-press-2017-09-20`); 9 October 2020: a trading-engine
  disconnection at 11:56 with no resumption (`press-elceo-2020-10-09`). Both are `EARLY_CLOSE`
  early terminations and both days count as sessions. The halts of 26 February 2016, April and
