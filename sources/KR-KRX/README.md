## Modelling decisions recorded against these sources

- **The exchange's own list is the authority.** KRX publishes a non-trading-day list for every
  year on its market-data site (휴장일, `krx-holidays`, the 2010-2027 responses preserved as JSON);
  the generated calendar reproduces all 268 listed weekdays 2010-2027 exactly and nothing else.
  The legal basis is 유가증권시장 업무규정 제5조 (`easylaw-trading-days`, cited by the exchange in
  `krx-kind-notice-99340`): public holidays under the Regulations on Public Holidays of Government
  Offices, Labour Day, Saturdays, 31 December (the preceding trading day when the 31st is a
  holiday or Saturday) and days the exchange designates. Days not on the list are trading days.
- **Substitute holidays are listed, not derived.** Korea's substitute-holiday system started in
  2014 for Seollal, Chuseok and Children's Day, was extended in August 2021 to the four national
  days, in May 2023 to Buddha's Birthday and Christmas, and in May 2026 to the newly restored
  Constitution Day and the new Labour Day public holiday; Seollal and Chuseok substitute only a
  Sunday overlap while the others also substitute a Saturday, and New Year's Day and Memorial Day
  never substitute (`moi-public-holidays-decree` art. 3, `mpm-substitute-holiday-history`). Rather
  than encode these shifting rules, the calendar uses `weekend_shift_policy: NONE` and lists every
  substitute day from the exchange's lists (each holiday module names them).
- **Overlaps carry one row.** 3 October 2017 was both National Foundation Day and the eve of
  Chuseok, and 5 May 2025 both Children's Day and Buddha's Birthday; each date is emitted once
  under the name the exchange's list gives it, with the substitute (6 October 2017, 6 May 2025)
  under the holiday the exchange attributes it to.
- **Labour Day.** A KRX closure every year under the exchange's rule although not a public
  holiday before 2026 (so a weekend 1 May brought no substitute); a public holiday with substitute
  days from 1 May 2026 (`mt-2026-04-28-holidays`), first applied on Monday 3 May 2027.
- **Election and temporary holidays.** Every election day since 2010 closed the market
  (term-expiry elections are public holidays; the early presidential elections of 9 May 2017 and
  3 June 2025 were designated temporary public holidays, `eurex-circular-4443920`), as did each
  temporary public holiday the government declared (14 August 2015, 6 May 2016, 2 October 2017,
  17 August 2020, 2 October 2023, 1 October 2024, 27 January 2025). 10 October 2025 and
  28 September 2026 were discussed but not designated (`wikitree-2026-09-28-not-designated`) and
  the market traded.
- **Year-end closure.** The exchange's own closure on 31 December, or the preceding trading day
  when the 31st is a Saturday (Friday 30 December 2011, 2016, 2022) or Sunday (Friday 29 December
  2017, 2023); listed as 연말휴장일 and excluded from settlement.
- **No early closes.** The regular session is 09:00-15:00, extended to 09:00-15:30 from 1 August
  2016 (`seoul-2016-07-31-hours`), on every trading day. The first trading day of the year opens
  at 10:00 with the normal close (`samsungpop-notice-23456`, `sedaily-2025-12-18-year-end`) and
  the college entrance exam day shifts the whole session one hour later (10:00-16:30 since 2016);
  both are session structure, out of scope, and neither shortens the day.
- **Unscheduled exceptions.** None since 2010. The exchange opened normally on 4 December 2024
  after the overnight martial-law declaration (`sbsbiz-2024-12-04-open`); the seven-minute
  KOSPI-wide matching halt of 18 March 2025 was intraday and "the first since the exchange's
  2005 integration" (`newsis-2025-03-18-halt`); the March 2020 circuit breakers were intraday.
  The exchange's lists are its record of every non-trading day, so the scope is `VERIFIED` to the
  retrieval date; no KRX-published monthly trading-day count was retrievable (data.krx.co.kr
  serves only browser sessions), so there is no independent count reconciliation on file.
- **`coverage.verified_through`: 2026-12-31**; the 2027 list is served by the exchange's endpoint
  but not yet offered in its year selector, so 2027 is `PROJECTED`.
