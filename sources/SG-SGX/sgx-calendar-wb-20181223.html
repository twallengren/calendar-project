Securities Trading
Trading Hours

Mid-day Break

From 13 November 2017, there will be a mid-day trading break from 1200h to 1300h, during which orders will not be matched.  Therefore, continuous trading will be divided into two sessions: a morning session from 0900h to 1200h and an afternoon session from 1300h to 1700h.

The mid-day break consists of two phases: a pre-open phase and a non-cancel phase.

  • The pre-open phase starts from 1200h and ends randomly within a one-minute window between 1258h to 1259h. If an investor has orders queuing in the order book prior to the start of the mid-day break, these existing orders will continue to rest in the order book.  Investors can continue to manage their orders, i.e. submit new orders, modify or cancel existing orders but there will not be order matching.  SGX will publish the Indicative Equilibrium Price (IEP), which is the price at which orders would be executed if auction matching was to occur at that point, to give investors an indication of the market level.
  • The non-cancel phase follows immediately after the pre-open phase ends. Investors will no longer be able to enter, modify or cancel orders, and orders will be matched at a single price derived by the same algorithm used to calculate the IEP. Continuous trading in the afternoon trading session starts at 1300h.  Orders which are not matched at the end of the mid-day break will be carried over to the afternoon trading session.

The introduction of a mid-day break addresses the preference of some market participants for shorter trading hours while retaining significant overlap in trading hours with key markets in Asia. In addition, the break may bring other benefits such as allowing trading representatives to better engage their clients or for issuers to make company announcements.
