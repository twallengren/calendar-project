Revised Dates for Hari Raya Puasa and Hari Raya Haji Public Holidays In 2022

21 October 2021 Employment practices

1 Following the announcement by the Majlis Ugama Islam Singapura (Muis) on the revised dates for Hari Raya Puasa and Hari Raya Haji next year, the Ministry of Manpower (MOM) has gazetted 3 May 2022 and 10 July 2022 as new public holidays for Hari Raya Puasa and Hari Raya Haji respectively.
