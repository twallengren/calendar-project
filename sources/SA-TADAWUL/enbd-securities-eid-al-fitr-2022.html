Holiday Notification - Eid Al Fitr 2022

28 April 2022

Dear Investor,

On the occasion of Eid Al Fitr, please note holidays for respective markets below:

  • Dubai Financial Market (DFM), Abu Dhabi Financial Exchange (ADX) and Nasdaq Dubai markets will observe holiday from Monday, 2nd May 2022 till Wednesday 4th of May 2022
  • The Saudi Stock Exchange - TADAWUL will observe holiday from Sunday, 01 May 2022 till Thursday 05th May 2022 and will resume officially on Sunday 08th May 2022

Financial settlement details for DFM, ADX and Nasdaq Dubai:

Trading Day Settlement Day
Thursday, 28th April 2022 Thursday, 05th May 2022
Friday, 29th April 2022 Friday, 06th May 2022

Financial settlement details for Tadawul:

Trading Day Settlement Day
Wednesday, 27th April 2022 Sunday, 08th May 2022
Thursday, 28th April 2022 Monday, 09th May 2022

We would like to take this opportunity to extend our best wishes.

Eid Mubarak.

Regards,
