# SA-TADAWUL sources

**Session-record reconciliation (13.0.0).** The exchange's own TASI history
(`saudi-exchange-tasi-sessions`, one row per trading day, 2020-01-01 to 2026-10-13) and its published
annual and quarterly trading-day counts (`saudi-exchange-statistical-reports`) reconcile with the
modelled Sunday-Thursday schedule on every day but five, all corrected in 13.0.0:

- **Eid al-Fitr 2021** ran from 11 May (the exchange's notice of 6 April 2021,
  `tadawul-ramadan-eid-notice-2021`: "Trading will discontinue at the end of trading day on 10th of
  May 2021 ... resume ... Monday 17th of May 2021"); earlier releases closed 13-16 May only,
  reading the Holidays-calendar entry's "first day 13/5, last day 16/5" as the closure rather than
  the Eid days.
- **Eid al-Fitr 2022** was 1-5 May only: the session record has full sessions on 28 April and
  8 May 2022, which the Holidays-calendar entry ("First day ... 28/4/2022. Last day ... 8/5/2022")
  named as the bounding trading days; `enbd-securities-eid-al-fitr-2022` corroborates. Earlier
  releases closed 28 April through 8 May.
- **23 November 2022** was an unscheduled closure, announced the evening before after the royal
  holiday for the World Cup win over Argentina (`saudi-exchange-closure-2022-11-23`); it is now
  modelled in `tadawul_unscheduled_closures`.

With these, the exchange's counts (251, 250, 248, 249, 250, 251 for 2020-2025; 183 through
September 2026) equal the schedule exactly. The 2 June 2021 market-wide halt
(`saudi-exchange-halt-2021-06-02`) and the 19 August 2026 delayed open
(`saudi-exchange-halt-2026-08-19`) kept full sessions and carry no row. `UNSCHEDULED_EXCEPTIONS`
is `VERIFIED` through 13 October 2026 and `PROJECTED` after.

## Modelling decisions recorded against these sources

- Eid closures are transcribed as explicit date ranges (the exchange closes for the whole
  announced period, weekend days included). Years beyond the last announcement are
  `status: PROJECTED` from the Umm al-Qura calendar using the spans the exchange has
  consistently announced: 28 Ramadan – 4 Shawwal (Eid al-Fitr) and 5 – 13 Dhu al-Hijjah
  (Eid al-Adha).
- National Day and Founding Day move to the nearest working day when they fall on a weekend,
  as the announcements for 2022, 2023 and 2025 show.
- The live exchange calendar never listed the 2020 Eid al-Adha holiday because the exchange
  published it as a market-news item, not on its Holidays page. That notice of 30 June 2020
  (`tadawul-eid-al-adha-2020-notice`, preserved from the Internet Archive together with the
  exchange's own tweet linking to it) fixes the closure at 29 July through 4 August 2020, with
  trading resuming Wednesday 5 August; the span was 8-14 Dhu al-Hijjah 1441, not the 5-13 pattern
  of later years. The 2020 National Day notice (`tadawul-national-day-2020-notice`) and the
  archived Holidays page (`tadawul-holidays-page-2020`, Eid al-Fitr) complete the year, and the
  full set of 2020 market-news items captured by the Internet Archive contains no other holiday
  notice, so `SCHEDULED_CLOSURES` is `VERIFIED` for 2020. COVID-19 shortened the 2020 session
  (10:00-13:00 from 26 March to 30 May) but closed no trading day.
