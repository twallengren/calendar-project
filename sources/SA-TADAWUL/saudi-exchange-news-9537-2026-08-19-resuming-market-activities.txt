Announcement from the Saudi Exchange on resuming market activities

Following the announcement published by the Saudi Exchange on the suspension of trading on Wednesday 19th of August 2026, the Saudi Exchange announces that trading will resume on Wednesday 19th of August 2026 with an auction that started at 10:15 AM and trading starts at 10:30 AM and all functions will be operating normally.

The Saudi Exchange apologizes for the inconvenience caused to market participants and continues to take all necessary measures to ensure market efficiency.
