## Modelling decisions recorded against these sources

- **Coverage starts in 2010.** No ASX document earlier than 2016 is served online and
  web.archive.org was unreachable when this calendar was built; the exchange's monthly
  trading-day counts (`asx-activity-report-*`) reach back to 2010 and the 14:10 early close is
  attested from December 2010 (`fnarena-2010-12-24`). Everything before 2010 would be a rule
  projection with no exchange evidence at all, so it is not published.
- **Which public holidays the exchange observes.** ASX Trade closes on the New South Wales
  public holidays that are also national: New Year's Day, Australia Day, Good Friday, Easter
  Monday, ANZAC Day, the Sovereign's Birthday (second Monday in June), Christmas Day and Boxing
  Day. It trades on NSW's Labour Day (first Monday of October: October 2020 had 22 trading days,
  every weekday) and on the August Bank Holiday, which NSW itself says "is not a declared public
  holiday" (`nsw-public-holidays-page`). Every surviving ASX Trade calendar (2020, 2022-2027)
  ends with the rule that "any weekday not included in this list" is a Business Day and a
  Trading Day (`asx-notice-0840-23-08`), so those years are `VERIFIED` for scheduled closures.
- **Weekend observance.** `NEXT_AVAILABLE_WEEKDAY` for the calendar: NSW adds a holiday on the
  following Monday when New Year's Day, Christmas Day or Boxing Day fall on a weekend, on the
  Tuesday when the Monday is already taken (Christmas on a Sunday: Monday 26 is Boxing Day and
  Tuesday 27 the additional day, as printed for 2022 and 2016), and moves Australia Day to the
  Monday outright. The exchange closes on exactly those days (`asx-trade-calendar-2022`:
  "Monday 3 January - Substitute for Saturday 1 January", "Tuesday 27 December - Substitute for
  Sunday 25 December"; `asx-trade-calendar-2020`: "As 26 Jan falls on a Sunday, the following
  Monday is observed"). Unlike TSX, the engine's default first-come ordering already yields the
  exchange's Monday/Tuesday pattern, so no `displaces` is needed.
- **ANZAC Day never moves - except twice.** `modules/holidays/au_anzac_day.yaml` uses
  `shift_policy: NONE`: a weekend ANZAC Day is a weekend day and the following Monday trades
  (27 April 2015, 27 April 2020, 26 April 2021: 20 trading days in each April). Two explicit
  additional closures sit inside coverage: Monday 26 April 2010 (ANZAC Day on a Sunday under the
  pre-2011 NSW regime; April 2010 had 19 trading days) and Tuesday 26 April 2011 (ANZAC Day on
  Easter Monday; April 2011 had 18), both from `asx-activity-report-2011-04`. 25 April 2011
  itself is emitted once, as Easter Monday. In 2026 and 2027 NSW gazetted additional Monday
  holidays after a weekend ANZAC Day (`nsw-public-holidays-page`, `asx-notice-0217-26-02`) and
  the exchange stayed open on both: the 2026 calendar has no 27 April row and April 2026 had 20
  trading days; the 2027 calendar prints "ANZAC Day Monday 26 April OPEN - Substitute for Sunday
  25 April". The calendar therefore follows the exchange, not the state, on those Mondays.
- **Sovereign's Birthday naming.** "Queen's Birthday" through 2022 and "King's Birthday" from
  2023 (the 2023 ASX calendar, printed in August 2022, still says Queen's Birthday; the 2024
  calendar says King's Birthday). The date rule is unchanged, so the two event sources only
  split the name.
- **Early closes: two regimes.** Through 2016 the exchange shortened "the last trading day before
  Christmas and the last trading day of the year" to 14:10 whatever their dates
  (`asx-notice-1514-16-12`: Friday 23 and Friday 30 December 2016 "in accordance with usual
  practice"), modelled with `shift_policy: PREVIOUS_AVAILABLE_BUSINESS_DAY`. From 2017 "ASX will
  only close early when the last trading day before Christmas and New Year's Holidays are the
  respective Eve of these holidays" (`asx-notice-1357-16-11`), so a weekend 24 or 31 December
  produces no early close at all (22 and 29 December 2017, 23 and 30 December 2022, 22 and 29
  December 2023 all printed OPEN or "normal trading hours"), modelled with the default `DROP`.
  exchange_calendars still applies the old behaviour in 2017, 2023 and 2028 (allowlisted). The
